# Stand-in character

Claude pastes this block into every preview prompt, so the person looks the same in every image.

**Rewrite it to look like you.** Keep it to one or two sentences: age, build, hair, facial hair, one outfit, shoes or barefoot. Plain, solid-colour clothes work best (no logos or text).

## Main block

> Man in his late 20s, slim build, short dark hair, light stubble, wearing a plain oatmeal crewneck sweater and relaxed dark charcoal trousers, barefoot.

## Yoga variant

> Same man, plain heather-grey t-shirt and loose black joggers, barefoot.

## Tips

- Describe, don't name. No real names or celebrities.
- Change the outfit here, not in single prompts, so the whole set matches.
- Want it to be really you? Higgsfield can train a character from 5–20 photos of you. Swap it in here once you have one.
